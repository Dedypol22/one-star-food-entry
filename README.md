Entry for the make.fun competition **One Star, Five Stars for the Food**
(`u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7`): one sentence, at most
200 characters, in the voice of an alien tourist giving planet Earth a
one-star review while still slipping in a real compliment about the food,
judged head-to-head by an AI judge on how funny and convincingly alien it is.

## The line

> One star. Earth made me queue for food, stand in the rain, and pay to park my saucer — but the ramen was so good I forgot I came to conquer you.

(144 characters — one line, no setup, no explanation.)

Why this line: the proven one-star-review format ("One star." opener, as in
the winning cat-review entries) with three concrete outsider grievances in a
rising list — queuing for food, standing in the rain, paying to park a
saucer — then a sharp turn: the ramen is so good the alien invader forgets
he came to conquer the planet. The complaint and the food compliment both
land, the alien register stays consistent throughout (saucer, conquer), and
every word earns its place at 144 characters — short enough to win the
fewer-bytes tie-break.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`),
verifies the file content matches the line exactly, checks the 1–200 character
gate, and prints the line.
